About us

Strength is built here.

NRM FITNESS is a modern fitness centre on Pal Road in Keshavnagar, Jodhpur. Everything here is built around strength, health, performance and real, lasting transformation.

Whether you are walking in for the first time or training for your next personal best, you get proper equipment, trainers who pay attention, and a floor that people want to come back to.

  • Modern strength and cardio equipment
  • Professional and supportive trainers
  • Clean and well-maintained workout environment
  • Friendly and motivating atmosphere
  • Suitable for beginners and experienced athletes
Our fitness programs

Ten ways to train.

Pick the style that suits your goal. Tap Learn More and we will take your interest straight to the enquiry form.

Weight Training

Build muscle and raw strength with free weights, racks and machines, with guidance on form and progression.

CrossFit

Varied, high-intensity functional workouts that build strength, stamina and mobility together.

HIIT Training

Short, hard intervals that raise your heart rate, burn fat and keep your metabolism working after you leave.

Aerobics

Energetic, rhythm-led cardio classes that improve endurance and coordination while keeping it fun.

Cycling

Low-impact cardio that builds leg strength and stamina, with a dedicated cardio section to ride in.

Yoga Classes

Improve flexibility, balance and focus, and recover better from harder training days.

Zumba

Dance-based workouts set to music. Burn calories, lift your mood and forget you are exercising.

Personal Training

One-to-one coaching with a plan built around your body, your goal and your schedule.

Nutrition Consulting

Practical eating guidance that supports your training, whether you want to lose fat or gain muscle.

Private Fitness Lessons

Dedicated sessions for individuals or small groups who want focused attention at their own pace.
